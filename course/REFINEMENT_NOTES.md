## Design decisions

- No attention animation merely for decoration.
- U/J concepts use small SVG diagrams because spatial shape materially improves comprehension.
- The arithmetic problem remains a ledger rather than a diagram.
- Q/K/V mathematics is collapsed under “Go deeper” so entry-level learners can skip it.
- “J curve” is not taught as a universal LLM law.
